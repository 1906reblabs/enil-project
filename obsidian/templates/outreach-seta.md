## Demo Agenda (15-minute call)

```
1. [2 min]  Context: what ENIL is and why the gap table exists
2. [5 min]  Walk through the demand gap table live (screen share)
            — Focus on their specific sector/OFO codes
            — Show vacancy source data (IDP + MERSETA + live scrape)
            — Show TVET graduate output by college
3. [3 min]  How they would use this for learnership allocation
4. [3 min]  Product options: quarterly report vs annual contract
5. [2 min]  Next steps
```
