# TVET College Outreach Template

← [[Revenue-Hub|← Revenue Hub]]

> **Key insight:** TVET principals are not buying data. They are buying a marketing asset that demonstrates graduate employment outcomes to DHET — which directly affects their funding. Lead with the outcome, not the product.

---

## Initial Outreach Email

**Subject:** Employment Outcomes Data for [COLLEGE NAME] — Demonstration

```
Dear Principal [NAME],

ENIL has been tracking live vacancy data across the Ekurhuleni industrial
corridor — specifically for the programmes your college offers.

The headline figure: your Electrical Engineering graduates face approximately
[X] open positions in the Springs/Germiston zone right now. Your Mechanical
Engineering graduates face [Y] vacancies. This data exists publicly — your
college just doesn't have it formatted in a way that communicates your
employment outcomes to prospective learners or to DHET.

We build Employment Outcomes Dashboards for TVET colleges: a branded microsite
in your college's colours showing live demand data for your specific programmes,
updated quarterly from our pipeline.

I'd like to show you a prototype specifically built for [COLLEGE NAME]'s
programmes. Available for a 20-minute call this week?

[YOUR NAME]
ENIL Navigation Intelligence
enil.co.za/ekurhuleni-map/
```

## Demo Agenda (20-minute call)

```
1. [3 min]  Show prototype dashboard (screen share)
            → "This is what we built for your college using public data"
2. [5 min]  Explain the problem it solves
            → Prospective learners can't find employment outcomes
            → DHET wants evidence of graduate demand
            → No TVET college in Ekurhuleni has this today
3. [4 min]  Walk through specific data points
            → Your Electrical graduates vs [X] vacancies in Springs
            → Salary comparison: your N6 graduate vs BCom graduate
4. [3 min]  Product: what they get
            → Branded microsite at college-name.enil.co.za/outcomes/
            → Quarterly updates from live data
            → They provide graduate survey data in exchange (feeds back to ENIL)
5. [3 min]  Pricing: R25k setup + R8k/year
6. [2 min]  Next steps: can we move to a formal proposal?
```
