// How to Access These Pathways

Artisan Pathway — 5 Steps

01

Subject Choice (Grade 9/10)

Technical Mathematics + Physical Sciences opens N1-N6 engineering. No Maths Literacy — it forecloses this pathway entirely.

02

TVET College (N1–N3)

Enrol at Springs, Ekurhuleni West, or Ekurhuleni East TVET. Apply for NSFAS — TVET is fully covered. No upfront fees.

03

TVET College (N4–N6)

Continue to N4-N6 which includes 18 months of workplace experience. Your employer covers the practical component.

04

SETA Apprenticeship

MERSETA (mechanical/electrical) or EWSETA (electrical) funds your formal apprenticeship. You earn while training.

05

Trade Test

Pass the Red Seal trade test through NAMB. You are now a certified artisan. Median salary: R35k–R47k/month. Ekurhuleni shortage means immediate employment.
