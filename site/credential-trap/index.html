⛔ The Single Most Damaging Subject Choice in SA Education

Mathematical Literacy is not a "softer maths" — it is a permanently disqualifying subject for both university engineering and TVET artisan pathways. Teachers who recommend it are optimising for pass rates, not learner outcomes.

Mathematical Literacy → FORECLOSES

  • All university engineering (BSc, BEng)
  • All NATED N1–N3 artisan programmes
  • Physical Sciences (most schools)
  • BCom Accounting (most institutions)
  • Any career requiring quantitative analysis

Technical Mathematics → OPENS

  • TVET N1–N6 Electrical Engineering
  • TVET N1–N6 Mechanical Engineering
  • MERSETA apprenticeships
  • NCV Level 4 engineering programmes
  • R35k–R47k/month within 5 years
