The Six NSFAS States

Every applicant is in one of these states. Find yours — then follow the exact action for that state.

0
Pre-Application
October – November
DEADLINE CRITICAL

The myNSFAS portal opens for new applications. This window closes — missing it means waiting an entire year. The portal is most unstable during peak submission days.

DOCUMENTS REQUIRED

  • Certified ID (not older than 3 months)
  • Proof of income — SASSA letter, payslips, or sworn affidavit
  • Institutional application confirmation number
⚠️ If the portal crashes: screenshot your reference number. Do NOT resubmit — duplicates are rejected with code R06.
1
Submitted / Pending
November – January
WAITING

Your application is submitted. NSFAS is processing. The portal is under extreme load. This is a passive state — your only task is to keep your reference number safe and monitor weekly.

WHAT TO DO

  • Save your submission confirmation screenshot
  • Note your reference number in a safe place
  • Check portal weekly — do NOT check daily (adds load)
  • Do not resubmit under any circumstances
2
Provisionally Funded
January – February
ACTION REQUIRED

NSFAS has approved you conditionally. Conditions are often not clearly communicated. Read every notification carefully — missing a condition submission converts this to a rejection.

COMMON CONDITIONS

  • Proof of registration at your institution
  • Academic record (returning students only)
  • Updated income documents if threshold changed
3
Appeals Window ← MOST CRITICAL
March – May
HIGHEST STAKES

You have received a rejection. The 30-day appeal window is now open. The single most important action: identify your rejection code (R01–R07) and cite the correct appeal grounds. See the rejection code table below.

APPEAL PROCESS — EXACTLY

  • Step 1: Get your rejection letter from myNSFAS — note the code (R01–R07)
  • Step 2: Match the code to the correct grounds in the table below
  • Step 3: Download the appeal letter template — fill in your specific details
  • Step 4: Attach supporting documents (listed per code below)
  • Step 5: Submit via myNSFAS appeals portal within 30 days of rejection date
✓ ~43% of correctly-grounded appeals succeed. Most failures are procedural, not factual.
4
Disbursement Active
February – November
FUNDED

Funds are flowing. Allowance disputes and tuition disputes have different resolution paths. Using the wrong path doubles your waiting time.

TWO DISTINCT DISPUTE PATHS

  • Allowance not received: Call 0800 067 327 → wait 7 business days → escalate to institution financial aid
  • Tuition not paid: Contact institution financial aid office → they escalate directly to NSFAS (you cannot do this yourself)
  • Never use the allowance path for tuition disputes — it creates a circular referral
5
Continuation (Year 2+)
September – October
RENEWAL REQUIRED

You must reapply each year. Academic progress thresholds vary by institution. Missing your institution's threshold — even by one module — triggers a rejection.

THRESHOLDS (2025)

  • Universities: pass at least 50% of registered modules per year
  • TVET colleges: pass at least 60% of subjects per level
  • Supplementary exams count — but results must be loaded before the renewal deadline

The Seven Rejection Codes

⚠️ Most appeals fail because applicants cite generic grounds ("I was unfairly rejected") instead of the specific grounds that apply to their code. The table below gives you the exact grounds NSFAS's own appeals adjudicators look for.
CodeOfficial ReasonCorrect Appeal Grounds + Documents
R01
 Income exceeds threshold Submit updated SARS ITR12 or sworn affidavit of changed income. Request formal financial re-assessment citing Section 5 of the NSFAS Act. Include any new unemployment / retrenchment documentation.
R02
 Not SA citizen or permanent resident Submit certified SA ID (green ID book or smart card) + unabridged birth certificate. If you have a Section 22 permit, include certified copy. Appeals on citizenship grounds require DIRCO verification.
R03
 Already receiving another bursary Submit a letter from the bursary provider confirming it covers only partial costs (not full cost of study). Specify which costs it covers and what gap remains. NSFAS may co-fund the uncovered portion.
R04
 Academic record insufficient Submit certified official transcript. If fail was due to death, illness, or crisis — submit compassionate grounds letter with supporting documentation (death certificate, hospital letter, etc.). Academic advisor statement helps.
R05
 Programme not on NSFAS-funded list Check the DHET-approved list of funded qualifications at dhet.gov.za. Ask your institution to re-register the programme if it qualifies. Submit confirmation from the institution that the programme is being registered.
R06
 Duplicate application detected Submit written confirmation from NSFAS of which application is active. Request deactivation of the duplicate via the call centre (0800 067 327) and get a reference number for the deactivation request. Include both reference numbers in your appeal.
R07 ★
 Income not verified by SARS / SASSA This is the most commonly mishandled code. Submit your SARS ITR12 tax return or a SASSA grant verification letter. If neither is available — submit a sworn affidavit from a commissioner of oaths (free at most police stations). Request a manual income assessment in your appeal letter.
