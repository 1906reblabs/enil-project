The Three Non-Obvious Truths
R45k
Artisan monthly earnings at year 5 vs R28k for BCom graduates in the same period. South Africa has a surplus of BCom graduates and a critical shortage of certified electricians, millwrights, and instrumentation technicians.
75%
NSC graduates with no viable funded pathway. DBE issues 600,000+ matric certificates. Universities accept ~200,000. NSFAS can fund fewer than 150,000 without collapse. The overflow is structural, not temporary.
~43%
NSFAS appeals success rate when the correct rejection code grounds are cited. Most appeals fail not on merit — but because the applicant doesn't know which grounds apply to their rejection code.
#1
Most damaging single subject choice: Mathematical Literacy in Grade 9. It permanently forecloses all university engineering programmes AND all NATED N1-N3 artisan pathways — the highest-demand, highest-paying TVET route in Ekurhuleni.
